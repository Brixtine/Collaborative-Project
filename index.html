Grade Calculator

Enter your average scores (0-100) for the Midterm components. The Midterm grade is calculated based on: Exam (30%), Project (40%), Attendance (10%), Participation (Quizzes/Assignments/Recitation Average - 20%).

Step 2: Calculate Required Final Term Grade.
Enter your desired Final GWA for the entire course (0-100). The calculator assumes the Final GWA = 50% Midterm Grade + 50% Final Term Grade.
